# 参考资料

## 电路与实时仿真工具

- [LiveSPICE](https://www.livespice.org/)：实时 SPICE-like 音频电路仿真及 Bassman 5F6-A 示例。
- [Online Circuit Solver](https://onlinecircuitsolver.com/)：MNA、拉普拉斯传递函数和线性电路公式检查。
- [NodalDKFramework](https://github.com/jardamacak/NodalDKFramework)：MATLAB Nodal DK 研究框架。
- [dkmethod](https://github.com/joaorossi/dkmethod)：JUCE Nodal DK 示例实现。

## 核心论文

1. Dempwolf, Holters, Zölzer, “Discretization of Parametric Analog Circuits for Real-Time Simulations,” DAFx-10, 2010. [PDF](https://www.dafx.de/paper-archive/2010/DAFx10/DempwolfHoltersZoelzer_DAFx10_P7.pdf)
2. Dempwolf, Zölzer, “A Physically-Motivated Triode Model for Circuit Simulations,” DAFx-11, 2011. [PDF](https://dafx.de/paper-archive/2011/Papers/76_e.pdf)
3. Yeh, Smith, “Discretization of the ’59 Fender Bassman Tone Stack,” DAFx-06, 2006. [PDF](https://dafx.de/paper-archive/2006/papers/p_001.pdf)
4. Holters, Zölzer, “Physical Modelling of a Wah-wah Effect Pedal as a Case Study for Application of the Nodal DK Method to Circuits with Variable Parts,” DAFx-11, 2011. [DAFx Archive](https://www.dafx.de/paper-archive/search?p=1&years%5B%5D=2011)
5. Holmes, van Walstijn, “Improving the Robustness of the Iterative Solver in State-Space Modelling of Guitar Distortion Circuitry,” DAFx-15, 2015. [PDF](https://dafx.de/paper-archive/2015/DAFx-15_submission_18.pdf)
6. Dunkel et al., “The Fender Bassman 5F6-A Family of Preamplifier Circuits—A Wave Digital Filter Case Study,” DAFx-16, 2016. [PDF](https://www.dafx.de/paper-archive/2016/dafxpapers/37-DAFx-16_paper_53-PN.pdf)
